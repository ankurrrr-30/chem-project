01 / A changing coastline

Every coastline has a story. Every community deserves time to prepare.

A tsunami is a series of long waves that can be triggered by sudden movement of the seafloor. This project looks at how coastal geography and community readiness shape risk—and how observing the ocean can help authorities issue advisories.
